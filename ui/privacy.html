Privacy in your workspace

Your data stays under your control.

Spend Control is a self-hosted advertising workspace operated by the organization that runs this website. The workspace operator controls the application, its server, and its backups.

What Facebook shares

When you connect Facebook, the workspace receives your app-scoped Facebook identity and name, your authorization and expiry information, and the business assets you authorize. These may include ad accounts, Pages, connected Instagram accounts, advertising results, pixels and lead forms. Your Facebook password is never received by this application.

How the information is used

Your identity restricts owner sign-in. Business access supports the brands and advertising workflows you configure. Access tokens and Page credentials are encrypted on the server. The browser receives connection status and asset names, not access tokens.

If you configure a creative provider, brand briefs and production inputs are sent to that provider. If you configure a CRM destination, lead data is delivered to it. These integrations use the credentials and destinations supplied by the workspace operator.

Ad comments and page intelligence

If engagement is enabled, the workspace stores accessible ad comments, author information returned by Meta, reply history, and public destination-page text. The configured MiniMax or Z.AI model receives relevant comment text, limited conversation context, ad copy, and page excerpts to draft and check responses. API keys, Facebook tokens, and commenter account IDs are not included in model prompts. Replies can be posted publicly according to the brand’s engagement settings. The workspace operator controls those settings and daily allowances.

Storage and removal

The workspace retains business records until the operator removes them. Disconnect advertising in Connections to pause managed delivery and remove the active Facebook advertising connection. Facebook owner sign-in stays linked so you can reconnect.

A verified Facebook deauthorization or data-deletion request removes the linked Facebook profile, authorization, cached asset inventory and Page credentials, and deletes stored leads, conversion submissions, comments, and page knowledge. Campaign, creative and financial records are retained for the workspace operator, including campaign identifiers needed to recover pending delivery stops. This application does not delete records held by Meta, your CRM, or other configured providers.

Requests and backups

Contact the organization that provided this workspace for deletion of remaining business records or copies in its backups. Backup retention and access are controlled by that operator. See the data-removal page for the available controls.
