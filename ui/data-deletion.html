Disconnect advertising

Open Connections and choose Disconnect advertising. Spend Control first requests pauses for managed campaigns. It removes the active advertising connection only after those pauses are confirmed. Owner sign-in remains available.

Remove the Facebook integration

Remove this app from your Facebook business integrations to revoke its authorization. Meta can send a signed deauthorization or data-deletion request to this workspace. The workspace removes the linked Facebook identity, authorization, cached assets and Page tokens, and stored leads, conversion submissions, comments, and page knowledge.

Removing authorization may prevent the app from pausing campaigns that are already running. Check delivery directly in Meta Ads Manager. Restoring workspace access after profile removal requires a recovery password or the setup token on your server.

Other business records

Campaign, creative and financial records belong to the workspace operator and remain available for reporting and delivery recovery. Contact the organization that operates this workspace to remove those records and any backups. Deletion here does not remove information already sent to Meta, a CRM, or another provider.
